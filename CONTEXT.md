# Health

Personal health planning for Adrian: getting strong, staying pain-free, and building the habits around both. Terms below are the canonical vocabulary for the wayfinder map, its tickets, and the plans they produce.

## Language

**Episode**:
An acute back-pain event severe enough to limit normal movement for days (most recent: 2026-03-20, ~1 week of limited movement). Historically preceded by sitting marathons and late-night streaks, not by lifting.
_Avoid_: injury, attack, flare-up

**Flare-up**:
A bad patch milder than an Episode — pain rises but movement stays possible. The deload protocol (fog) is about Flare-ups; professional review is about Episodes.
_Avoid_: episode (reserved for the severe events)

**The provocation**:
The one movement that currently reproduces pain: posterior pelvic tilt (tucking the pelvis back out of the habitual belly-forward stance) → left-sided lower-back pain, worse with abdominal bracing added in that position. Bracing in the habitual stance is painless. The #1 question for the next physio visit.
_Avoid_: pain on bracing (superseded description)

**Returning lifter**:
Adrian's training status — experienced technique from years of past gym time, detrained capacity from the gap. Means: no instruction needed, but the loading gates from the training research still apply.
_Avoid_: beginner, novice

**Recomp block**:
The first 8-week phase: maintenance calories with protein at 1.6–2.2 g/kg, letting training drive muscle regain without buying fat. Reassessed by weight trend and mirror at the end of the block.
_Avoid_: bulk, cut, surplus

**Late-night streak**:
A multi-day run of staying up to 2–3 am (~once per 2–3 months). The historical Episode precursor and the target of the sleep guardrail — sleep is otherwise a healthy baseline to protect, not a deficit to fix.
_Avoid_: sleep deprivation (implies a current chronic state that doesn't exist)
