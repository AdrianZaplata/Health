# 007 — Gym program draft (v1)

Prototype for [Draft the gym program](https://github.com/AdrianZaplata/Health/issues/8). **Status: draft — awaiting Adrian's reactions.** Built on the [training research](https://github.com/AdrianZaplata/Health/issues/4), the [constraints fact sheet](https://github.com/AdrianZaplata/Health/issues/6), and the [assessment outcome](https://github.com/AdrianZaplata/Health/issues/2) ("train to be stronger", no restrictions prescribed).

## Shape of the block

- **8 weeks, full-body, 3 sessions/week (A / B / C), 60–75 min.** Sessions are self-contained and order-flexible: do them on any days with at least one rest day between hinge-heavy sessions (B and C). **Bad-week floor: A + B only.**
- **Recomposition block**: maintenance calories, 145–200 g protein/day, creatine — owned by the [nutrition ticket](https://github.com/AdrianZaplata/Health/issues/9); listed here only so the training volume makes sense.
- You're an experienced lifter returning, not a novice: no technique-teaching phase. Weeks 1–2 are a deliberate **ramp** (2 working sets, ~3 reps in reserve) to re-grease patterns and let tendons catch up to muscle memory. Weeks 3–7 run the progression at 3 sets. Week 8 is a light **reassess week**: retest the endurance gate, review pain diary, decide block 2.
- **Progression rule (all exercises): double progression.** Work within the rep range; when you hit the top of the range on all sets with ~1–2 reps in reserve, add 2.5 kg (upper body) or 5 kg (lower body) next session. If a session is bad, repeat the weight — no forcing.

## The two safety rails (from the research + current pain picture)

1. **No loaded end-range posterior pelvic tilt** until the physio characterizes the provocation. Concretely: no tuck-and-squeeze cue at hip-thrust lockout (finish **neutral**, glutes on, no tail-tuck), stop squat/leg-press depth **above** butt-wink, no loaded end-range lumbar flexion (drops the decline sit-ups from your first session back — replaced by the trunk block).
2. **Hinge gate**: barbell deadlift from the floor waits until (a) Biering–Sørensen hold ≥ 60 s, (b) pain no worse than mild and settled within 24 h after sessions, (c) trap-bar pulls feel crisp. Until then the hinge lives as Romanian deadlifts and trap-bar (high-handle) deadlifts — which are real training, not rehab filler. Same logic gates the **back squat** behind pain-free goblet → front-squat progression; most lifters clear it in 2–4 weeks.

**Sørensen self-test** (retest end of week 4 and week 8): torso off a bench edge at hip height, ankles anchored, arms crossed, hold horizontal until form breaks. ≥ 60 s = gate open.

## Purpose tags

Every exercise carries why it's there, so trade-offs are arguable: **[S]** strength, **[P]** the posture/back goal (extensor endurance, hip strength, position tolerance — *not* "correcting the tilt", which the evidence doesn't support), **[L]** looking good.

## Session B — hinge focus

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Tags | Notes |
|---|----------|-------------|------|-------|
| 1 | Trap-bar deadlift (high handles) → low handles → barbell from floor | 3 × 5–8 | S, P | Floor pull only after the Sørensen gate; trap bar cuts lumbar moment vs straight bar |
| 2 | Overhead press (standing, ribs down) | 3 × 6–10 | S, L | If lumbar hyperextension creeps in, switch to seated or half-kneeling landmine |
| 3 | Lat pulldown → pull-ups | 3 × 8–12 | S, L | |
| 4 | Hip thrust | 3 × 8–12 | P, L, S | **Neutral finish — no posterior-tilt squeeze at lockout** (that's your exact provocation) |
| 5 | Back extension (45°), pause at top | 2 × 10–15 | P | Direct extensor endurance → drives the Sørensen gate |

## Week-by-week

| Weeks | What changes |
|-------|--------------|
| 1–2 | Ramp: 2 working sets everywhere, ~3 reps in reserve, re-find working weights. Baseline Sørensen test in week 1 (don't grind it). |
| 3–4 | 3 sets, double progression live. Squat variant progresses if clean. Sørensen retest end of wk 4. |
| 5–7 | Progression continues. If gates passed: low-handle trap bar → barbell pulls; back squat enters. If not: keep progressing the current variants — they build the same qualities. |
| 8 | Reassess: −30–40% load week, Sørensen retest, pain-diary review, physio's posterior-tilt answer folded in → block 2 gets planned (currently fog on the map). |

## What this draft deliberately leaves out

- **Rehab/mobility protocol** — its own [open ticket](https://github.com/AdrianZaplata/Health/issues/7); the trunk blocks here are placeholders it may re-own.
- **Flare-up/deload rules** beyond "repeat the weight" — map fog, unblocking after this resolves.
- Cardio/steps — nothing decided anywhere yet; raise it if you want it in.
