## Question

The pain narrative names two amplifiers that no gym program touches: sleep deprivation, and the sitting habits that started this (right-over-left leg crossing, leaning left, long uninterrupted sitting). Decide the concrete countermeasures.

To settle:

- **Sleep**: given what the grilling reveals is actually causing the deprivation, which changes are committed to (schedule, environment, wind-down, screens), and what's the target hours range.
- **Sitting habits**: what replaces the leg-cross-and-lean default — desk/chair changes, a break cadence (and what triggers it), position rotation. Habit changes must be few and specific enough to stick, not a list of twenty ergonomic virtues.
- **Which habit comes first**: sequencing, since attempting everything at once is how habit changes fail.
