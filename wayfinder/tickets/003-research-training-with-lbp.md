## Question

What do high-trust sources say about how a beginner with a history of recurrent low back pain should structure their first months of gym training?

Specifically:

1. Is progressive resistance training safe and effective for people with chronic/recurrent low back pain, per the evidence?
2. What established frameworks exist for building spine tolerance first (e.g. McGill's "Big 3", hip-hinge patterning progressions), and what does the evidence say about them?
3. Which common gym movements are typically modified, delayed, or substituted early on for this population, and what are the standard progressions back to full versions?
4. What does a sensible beginner template look like (frequency, full-body vs split, volume, progression scheme) per strength-training literature — independent of the back issue?
5. How is glute/hip strengthening typically programmed for people whose pain narrative involves hip-flexor tightness and glute inhibition?

Findings go to `wayfinder/research/003-training-with-lbp.md`, every claim cited to its primary source.
