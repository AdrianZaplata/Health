## Question

Book and complete an in-person assessment with a physiotherapist (or sports-medicine doctor) so the self-diagnosis becomes a verified picture, and gym training gets explicit clearance. This is a task ticket: nothing to decide, but every programming decision downstream is blocked until it's done.

Why it can't be skipped: 2–3 past episodes severe enough to prevent movement for days, plus current pain in the left lower back when tightening the abdominal muscles, are exactly the findings a professional should look at before the spine gets loaded in a gym.

Checklist for Adrian:

- [ ] Book a physiotherapist or sports-medicine assessment (mention: 7+ years of asymmetric sitting, 2–3 acute immobilizing episodes, current pain on abdominal bracing, left lower back).
- [ ] Ask them to verify or refute the working hypothesis: lateral pelvic tilt, pelvic torsion, inhibited glutes, shortened hip flexors.
- [ ] Ask specifically about the pain on abdominal bracing — what is it, and does it change how core work should be done?
- [ ] Ask for a red-flag screen given the immobilizing episodes.
- [ ] Get explicit clearance for strength training: which movements are fine now, which are restricted or need modification, and for how long.
- [ ] Collect any prescribed exercises so the rehab protocol ticket can build around them instead of alongside them.

The resolution records: clinical findings, cleared movements, restricted movements, prescribed rehab work, and recommended follow-up.
