## Question

Pin down the facts about Adrian's life that every downstream plan must fit. A live `/grilling` session covering at least:

- **Training logistics**: which gym (or joining one?), what equipment it has, how many days per week are realistically sustainable, session length, time of day.
- **Body baseline**: age, height, weight, current activity level, training history (any lifting before?).
- **Work setup**: the chair/desk where the 7 years of leaning happened — what can change there, how many hours a day seated, can breaks be taken.
- **Pain picture today**: current pain frequency/intensity day-to-day, known triggers, what the last acute episode looked like and when.
- **Sleep situation**: what's actually causing the sleep deprivation (schedule? environment? habits? kids? screens?), current hours, what's changeable.
- **Food**: preferences, restrictions, allergies, cooking capacity, appetite, budget.
- **Budget and access**: physiotherapy cost/coverage, willingness to pay for recurring sessions.

The resolution is a fact sheet these tickets consume: [Draft the gym program](007-draft-gym-program.md), [Decide the nutrition plan](008-decide-nutrition-plan.md), [Decide the sleep and daily-habit changes](009-decide-sleep-and-habits.md).
