## Question

Given the professional assessment's findings and the research on evidence-based back rehab: what is the daily/weekly rehab and mobility protocol?

To settle:

- Which specific exercises (assessment's prescriptions first, evidence-based staples second), at what frequency, and how they interleave with gym days.
- How the pain-on-bracing finding shapes core work — what's in, what's out, what's progressed later.
- Hip flexor / glute work: what actually earns a place once the hypothesis has been professionally verified or corrected.
- The stop rule: what pain response during any exercise means "stop and modify" vs "expected discomfort".
