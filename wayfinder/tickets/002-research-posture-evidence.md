## Question

How well does the evidence support the working hypothesis (habitual leg crossing + leaning → lateral pelvic tilt / pelvic torsion → chronic low back pain), and what do current clinical guidelines actually recommend for chronic non-specific low back pain?

Specifically:

1. What does peer-reviewed evidence say about the link between postural asymmetries (pelvic tilt, leg crossing, sitting posture) and low back pain? Is the causal chain solid, contested, or refuted?
2. What is the clinical standing of "lower crossed syndrome" and "gluteal amnesia" — established diagnoses or contested constructs?
3. What do the major current guidelines (WHO 2023 low back pain guideline, NICE NG59, ACP, Lancet low back pain series) recommend for chronic non-specific low back pain — especially the role of exercise, and which kinds?
4. What red flags should send someone with recurrent immobilizing episodes to a doctor rather than a gym?

Findings go to `wayfinder/research/002-posture-evidence.md`, every claim cited to its primary source.
